# TerrainScout

Terrain-based heat map of where mule deer, elk, black bear and whitetail are most likely to be.

Open the app: https://eliguidroz.github.io/terrainscout/

Elevation from AWS Terrain Tiles, water from OpenStreetMap. The model scores slope, aspect,
elevation band, water and terrain shape. It doesn't know vegetation, private land or closures.
